Prologue: The New World

✳

A story by Barbie Vicenta & Uno

As the world we once knew drew its final breath, two souls aimlessly wandered in hopes of a new Earth.

Yet, beyond the stars is a world untouched by time — a forest beneath unfamiliar moons, with towering trees and the grandest fruits.

In this World Tree, the whisper of crushed green leaves and the splash of crispy, sweet ripened fruits unfold into a soft, blooming breeze. Along with it lies the quiet warmth of wood and Earth underneath, as if the planet itself had been eager to greet.

One world may have been escaped, yet a new, strange paradise opened its ancient gate.

And so a new chapter begins on the next page.
